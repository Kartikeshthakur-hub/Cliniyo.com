Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Draft for legal review. This page describes how this marketing website handles information. It should be reviewed by a qualified lawyer, and updated for Nepal’s applicable data-protection requirements, before launch.

What this website collects

When you request a demo, you may give us your name, clinic name, phone or WhatsApp number, email address, clinic type, number of branches and a short note. The request form on this website does not send these details to a server. It prepares an email in your own email app, and the details reach us only if you send that email.

How we use it

We use the details in your email to reply to your request, arrange a demo and discuss Cliniyo with you. We do not sell your details.

Fonts and third parties

This website loads typefaces from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google’s servers when the page loads. We do not run advertising trackers on this website.

Patient data

This policy covers this website only. Patient information that clinics store in the Cliniyo product is covered by the agreement between Cliniyo and each clinic.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete the details you sent us by emailing hello@cliniyo.com.
