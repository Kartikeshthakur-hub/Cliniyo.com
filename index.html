The problem

Most clinics run on a patchwork.

A paper register at the front desk, a spreadsheet for fees, a chat group for the queue, another tool for billing. Each one works alone. Together they cost hours and make every patient wait a little longer.

Records you can’t find

Visit history sits in registers and phone galleries. Finding what happened last time means searching.

Slower consultations, repeated questions.

A queue nobody can see

Walk-ins, booked patients and cancellations are juggled by memory or in a chat group.

Longer waits, a stressed front desk.

Fees tracked by hand

Partial payments and pending balances live in notebooks and spreadsheets.

Money owed and never collected.

Follow-ups left to memory

The patient who should return in three weeks is remembered only if someone remembers.

Unfinished treatment, lost repeat visits.

Staff and commissions by hand

Shifts, salaries, commissions and referral payouts are calculated at month end.

Disputes and hours of admin.

No clear view of the business

Owners ask for numbers and get them days later, one branch at a time.

Decisions made on guesswork.

The Cliniyo solution

Not clinic software. A Clinic OS.

One patient record, one visit flow, one source of truth. A patient checks in, waits, is seen, pays and books the next visit, and everyone sees the same picture without asking.

Patient Registration→Appointment→Waiting / Queue→Session→Consultation→Treatment→Billing→Payment→Follow-Up

  1. Patient Registration

    Create the patient once. Contact details, blood group and relevant information stay on one profile.

  2. Appointment

    Book against a doctor’s schedule, or add a walk-in. Cancellations and no-shows are recorded.

  3. Waiting / Queue

    Check the patient in and give them a place in the queue everyone can see.

  4. Session

    The visit itself, coordinated between reception, the doctor and the other staff involved.

  5. Consultation

    The doctor records notes, prescriptions and investigations on the patient’s record.

  6. Treatment

    Plan and record procedures, including treatments that span several visits.

  7. Billing

    Charges come from the service catalogue and become an invoice.

  8. Payment

    Record full or partial payments. Outstanding balances stay visible.

  9. Follow-Up

    Record the return date, see it when it’s due, and track whether the patient comes back.

A session is not just a consultation.

It’s the whole coordinated visit. The consultation is one part of it, alongside reception and any other staff involved.

The service catalogue is separate.

It holds your configured services, prices and durations. Treatment records what was actually done for the patient.

Security & trust

Patient data deserves careful handling.

Clinic records are private. Cliniyo is designed around controlling who can see what.

Role-based access

Branch-aware permissions

FAQ

Questions clinic owners ask.

Who is Cliniyo for?
Private clinics in Nepal, starting with dental, dermatology and skin, hair and aesthetic, general and small multi-specialty clinics. It’s designed for owners, doctors, receptionists, managers and finance staff, including people who are not technical.
Is Cliniyo only for dental clinics?
No. The core (patients, appointments, queue, consultation, treatment, billing, staff and follow-ups) works for any clinic. Specialty workflows are built as modules on top of that core.
What’s the difference between a session and a consultation?
A session is the whole coordinated visit between reception, the doctor and other staff involved. The consultation is the doctor’s part within it.
Does Cliniyo send WhatsApp and SMS messages automatically?
Messaging is available on the plans that include it, and depends on your channel setup, your clinic’s configuration, each patient’s preferences and applicable privacy requirements. Cliniyo prepares follow-up messages from your templates; we’ll show you exactly what is live for your clinic in the demo.
Does it work with eSewa or Khalti?
Not yet. Local payment providers are under consideration. We won’t list an integration as available until it has been built and tested.
Will AI diagnose patients?
No. Planned AI features on the Scale plan are limited to administrative tasks and workflow assistance. They are not available yet.
Can I limit what each staff member can see?
Yes. Access is role-based, so sensitive information such as finances and payroll is only visible to people with the right permissions.
Can I run more than one branch?
Yes. Growth supports 2 branches and Scale supports 5, with branch allocation and staff transfers.
Is there a free trial?
Choose “Demo and trial” on the request form and tell us about your clinic. We’ll confirm what’s available for you.
